Tutorial 01

Download it. Open it. Get started.

50 seconds · captioned

Follow the Windows installation walkthrough. The download page also includes builds for Apple Silicon, Intel Macs and Linux.

Download videoRead the full transcript
01 / DOWNLOAD

Choose the full desktop ZIP

Open the latest release, expand Assets, and choose veil-…-[your platform].zip.

02 / EXTRACT

Keep the folder together

On Windows, choose Extract All. Keep the app, bin/ and veil-install.txt together in a writable folder.

03 / OPEN

Launch the Veil

Open veil.exe on Windows. On macOS or Linux, run ./veil from the extracted folder.

The videos show v1.1.3. Builds are currently unsigned; follow your OS approval flow only for a release you trust. Managed devices may need administrator approval. Coding tools require Python, and some checks also require Node.

Tutorial 02

Connect Cloudflare AI. Send your first prompt.

62 seconds · captioned

Use Workers AI without downloading local model weights. You’ll need a Cloudflare account and an internet connection.

Download videoRead the full transcript
01 / CONNECT

Log in with Cloudflare

In Settings, scroll to Cloudflare login. Select Log in with Cloudflare, then sign in in your browser.

02 / REVIEW

Choose your permissions

Review identity, account and Workers AI access. Decline optional deployment, storage or domain permissions you don’t need.

03 / CHAT

Pick a model and say hello

Return to the Veil. Check Cloudflare Workers AI in Settings, choose an available model, then open Chat.

Try this: “In one short friendly sentence, suggest a first project for someone learning to build software.”
What did the real demo return?

“Why not start by building a simple to-do list app to get familiar with coding and see your progress come to life.”

This reply was returned through the Veil’s chat service using Cloudflare’s @cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast on 19 September 2026. The video typesets the reply to exclude private account information. The full test prompt also instructed the assistant not to use tools, memory, files or personal context.

Is Cloudflare AI free?

Cloudflare currently includes 10,000 neurons per day at no charge. Usage depends on the model and request; some models require paid billing. Check Cloudflare’s current pricing and your account usage. Pricing checked 19 September 2026.

What happens to my data?

Cloudflare AI processes your prompts remotely. Optional R2 access can enable backups of conversations and durable memories to your own Cloudflare account; R2 may need activation. Review the permissions before connecting. Disconnect through Settings when needed; existing R2 backups remain in your account.

What if it does not connect?

Finish the browser consent flow and return to the running app. Check that Settings shows Cloudflare connected, then choose an available Workers AI model. Quota or billing errors should be checked in your Cloudflare account. See the connection guide for more help. Cloudflare Tunnel is a separate feature and is not required for AI chat.
